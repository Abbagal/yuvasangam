01 The Itinerary

Seven days in Maharashtra.

Raj Bhavan, a day with an NGO in Dahanu, Mumbai Darshan, MMRDA's Atal Setu and Sea Link, and IIT Bombay's labs. Meals are in the hostel mess for students and at Padmavihar guest house for faculty and staff.
