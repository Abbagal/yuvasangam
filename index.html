01 About Yuva Sangam

One nation, understood first-hand — by its youth.

Yuva Sangam is a youth exchange programme under Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat (EBSB) — the Government of India's initiative to build mutual understanding between the people of different States and UTs.

First launched in 2023, it pairs Higher Education Institutions from two different States/UTs to run reciprocal exposure tours of 5–7 days — sending their own youth to the paired state, and hosting the paired state's youth in return.

It is built around experiential learning: direct, first-hand exposure to another part of India's diversity, explicitly aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.

02 The 5 Ps

Five lenses on a host state.

Every tour is designed to give delegates a rounded picture of the host state, across five themes.

  1. i. पर्यटन Paryatan Tourism
  2. ii. परंपरा Parampara Traditions
  3. iii. प्रगति Pragati Development
  4. iv. प्रौद्योगिकी Prodyogiki Technology
  5. v. परस्पर संपर्क Paraspar Sampark People-to-people connect
