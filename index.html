01 About Yuva Sangam

One nation, understood first-hand — by its youth.

Yuva Sangam is a youth exchange programme under Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat (EBSB) — the Government of India's initiative to build mutual understanding between the people of different States and UTs.

First launched in 2023, it pairs Higher Education Institutions from two different States/UTs to run reciprocal exposure tours of 5–7 days — sending their own youth to the paired state, and hosting the paired state's youth in return.

It is built around experiential learning: direct, first-hand exposure to another part of India's diversity, explicitly aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.

How we travel and where to assemble

We travel as one group: IITB bus to Mumbai Airport, then IndiGo to Srinagar. No one travels to the airport separately.

  1. Reach IIT Bombay on your own by 4:00 PM on 20 October. The nearest station is Kanjurmarg / Vikhroli on the Central Railway line. Report at the main gate desk.
  2. Stay in your allotted hostel for the night. Do not leave campus after the orientation.
  3. Be outside your hostel at 8:15 AM on 21 October with all luggage. The bus picks you up there.
  4. Assemble at the meeting point for the flag-off ceremony. Stay with your group leader.
  5. Bus leaves at 9:00 AM. Late arrivals will not be waited for.
  6. At the airport, follow the coordinators through bag drop and security as a group.

Gate passes for IIT Bombay entry will be sent to you in follow-up emails. Keep them on your phone and carry a printout.

Srinagar weather (20–27 Oct) and essentials to carry

Expect cold, dry weather: about 17–20°C in the afternoon and 4–7°C at night and early morning. Late October is one of the driest periods of the year, but evenings turn chilly fast and it can be colder at higher spots like Gulmarg or Sonamarg.

Dress in layers. Routine clothes alone will not be enough.

  • 1 heavy jacket (padded or fleece-lined)
  • 2 sweaters / sweatshirts or fleece
  • 2 thermal inner sets
  • Woollen cap, gloves, muffler
  • Woollen socks (3–4 pairs)
  • Comfortable closed walking shoes / sports shoes
  • Full-length clothing for 7 days; one set of formal or traditional wear for cultural events
  • Moisturiser, lip balm, sunscreen (dry air, strong sun)
  • Personal medicines for the full 7 days (any regular medicines, with prescription), plus basic medicines for cold, fever, headache, acidity and travel sickness
  • Water bottle, small torch, umbrella or light raincoat
  • Phone charger and power bank (cabin bag)
  • Small backpack for day trips

Documents, phones and valuables

Carry an original photo ID and photos; without a valid ID you will not be allowed to board.

Documents to carry

  • Original government photo ID: Aadhaar card (mandatory), plus PAN / Voter ID / Passport / Driving Licence if available
  • College ID card
  • 2 photocopies of your Aadhaar and college ID
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Gate pass (from email) and boarding pass – on phone and printed
  • Signed undertaking form (see below)

Mobile phones

Only postpaid SIM cards work in Jammu & Kashmir. Prepaid SIMs from other states do not work there. If you have only a prepaid connection, convert it to postpaid or arrange a postpaid SIM before 20 October. Share a parent's number with your coordinator as an emergency contact.

Valuables and jewellery

  • Do not carry valuables: expensive watches, gadgets you don't need, or large amounts of cash.
  • Do not carry jewellery. If you choose to carry any, it is entirely at your own risk; organisers are not responsible for loss or damage.
  • Carry a debit card / UPI and a small amount of cash for personal expenses.

Return on 27 October

The programme ends at Mumbai Airport on Tuesday, 27 October when our flight lands at 16:20. There is no bus back to IIT Bombay; you travel home directly from the airport.

  • Plan your onward train, bus or ride home in advance, keeping a buffer for flight delays.
  • Inform your coordinator before leaving the airport, and confirm on the WhatsApp group once you reach home.
  • Tell your family the expected landing time so someone can pick you up if needed.
